1. Core Design Philosophy: "Warm Passbook & Huggable Helper"
Most finance apps fail older adults and beginners because they look like stock-market terminals full of charts, percentages, and tiny icons. WealthOS v5.0 replaces complexity with warmth, trust, and physical simplicity:
100% Zero Jargon on Screen: Words like Debit, Credit, Liability, Equity, Amortization, XIRR, and Reconciliation are strictly hidden from the screen. The app speaks like a caring family member: "Money In", "Money Spent", "Safe to Spend Today", and "Kept Aside for Bills".
The "Only 3 Things on Screen" Rule: To prevent visual overwhelm, no screen ever displays more than 3 main sections at once.
Senior-Friendly & Child-Friendly Ergonomics:
Giant Pillow Buttons (64px+ height): Every button looks like a real, physical soft pillow button with a raised bottom edge so you always know what is clickable.
Big, Clear Words + Picture Icons: No mystery icons or hidden swipe gestures. Every button has a large colorful picture AND a clear label in bold text (e.g., 🎙️ Speak to Add, 📷 Take Bill Photo, ↩️ Undo Mistake).
100% Forgiving ("You Cannot Break Anything"): After any action, a big comforting yellow button stays at the bottom for 10 seconds: "Made a mistake? Tap here to Undo ↩️". Seniors never have to fear pressing the wrong button.
